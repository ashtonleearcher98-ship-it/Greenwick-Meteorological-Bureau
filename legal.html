01 · WEATHER INFORMATION

Forecasts and warnings

Forecasts on this site are generated by the Greenwick weather model from a cached outlook. The website shows a ten-day view from a longer forecast cache. Conditions, rainfall amounts and risk ratings are estimates and can change when the model is refreshed.

Weather warnings are model guidance calculated from forecast values such as rainfall, wind, heat, humidity, UV and fire danger. They are not a substitute for official emergency warnings, evacuation notices or directions from emergency services. For immediate danger, follow local emergency-service instructions.

Rainfall amounts are shown in millimetres (mm) as estimated accumulation for the selected forecast day. Rain probability is a separate estimate and does not guarantee that rain will occur at a particular place or time.

02 · TERMS OF USE

Using this website

You may use this website for personal information and planning. Do not treat model output as a guarantee, use it as the sole basis for a safety-critical decision, or present a model alert as an official emergency notice.

The Bureau may update, correct or temporarily withdraw site content. Forecast availability and uninterrupted access are not guaranteed.

03 · PRIVACY

Privacy and data

This site does not ask visitors to create an account, submit personal details or share precise location. Searching and selecting a forecast area happens in the browser. The site does not add advertising or analytics scripts. It loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive standard connection information when those fonts are requested.

As with other hosted websites, the hosting provider may process standard technical request data to deliver and protect the service under its own privacy and security practices.

04 · SITE CONTENT

Copyright and accessibility

Site text, design and Greenwick Bureau branding are provided for use with this service. If you reuse site material, include attribution to the Greenwick Meteorological Bureau and do not imply that model estimates are official observations.

The site is designed for keyboard and screen-reader access. If you encounter an accessibility issue, use the forecast and warning pages with their search and filter controls, or contact the site publisher through the channel where you received this website.
